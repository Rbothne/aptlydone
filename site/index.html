Monthly cost is the true monthly cost: rent spread over the lease after any free months stated for that unit, plus required monthly fees and required one-time fees spread over the lease. Parking and optional fees are not included.

Tags compare cost per square foot between units with the same number of bedrooms and a similar size. Building steal: 15% or more below similar units in the same building. Local value: 20% or more below similar units in other buildings within one to two miles. Absolute steal: 10% or more below its building and 15% or more below its neighbors. Check price: 30% or more below its own building, which is usually an income-restricted unit the building did not label, or a mistake; it is not counted as a steal. Prices come from each building's own site and are only as good as what it publishes; check with the building before relying on one.

Crime counts are incidents reported to police within half a mile in the past 12 months, from each county's open data (so far: none). Counties count offences differently, so compare across a border with care. About the area figures are for the census tract (American Community Survey, 5-year) and the ZIP code (Zillow's rent index).

Drive times are free-flow times with no traffic, so they compare buildings fairly but understate a rush-hour commute. Your work address is kept in this browser only. To time drives it is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to find its coordinates, and those coordinates are sent to the public OSRM routing server along with the buildings' locations.
