Monthly cost is the true monthly cost: rent spread over the lease after any free months stated for that unit, plus required monthly fees and required one-time fees spread over the lease. Parking and optional fees are not included.

Tags compare cost per square foot between units with the same number of bedrooms and a similar size. Building steal: 15% or more below similar units in the same building. Local value: 20% or more below similar units in other buildings within one to two miles. Absolute steal: 10% or more below its building and 15% or more below its neighbors. Prices come from each building's own site and are only as good as what it publishes; check with the building before relying on one.
